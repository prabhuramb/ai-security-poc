VACATION AND LEAVE POLICY (Sample)

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly.
Vacation requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance via the
HR portal. Unused vacation of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next
calendar year. Sick leave is separate and is granted at 10 days per year,
non-accruing.

REMOTE WORK POLICY (Sample)

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed annually.
